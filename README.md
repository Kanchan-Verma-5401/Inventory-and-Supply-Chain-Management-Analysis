📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing inventory performance, warehouse utilization, transportation costs, lead times, sales trends, and backorders to understand overall supply chain efficiency.

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to provide a consolidated view of key supply chain KPIs and identify areas where inventory and logistics performance can be improved.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze overall inventory performance and utilization.
Monitor Days Sales of Inventory (DSI) and Inventory Turnover Ratio.
Compare transportation costs across regions and product categories.
Analyze yearly sales trends.
Identify backorders based on order status.
Compare inventory levels across categories and regions.
Evaluate average lead time for different product categories.

📈 Dashboard Analysis
1. Warehouse Utilization
The dashboard tracks warehouse utilization and shows a value of 34.08, helping assess how effectively available warehouse capacity is being used.
2. Inventory Performance

Two important inventory metrics are analyzed:
Days Sales of Inventory: 15.56 days
Inventory Turnover Ratio: 23.47

These metrics help evaluate how quickly inventory is converted into sales and how much inventory is being maintained.

4. Sales Trend Analysis

Year-wise unit sales are visualized to identify changes in demand over time.

The dashboard shows a significant increase in units sold after 2021, reaching approximately 172K units in 2022, 195K in 2023, and around 198K in 2024.

5. Lead Time Analysis

Average lead time is compared across product categories:

Accessories: 16.60
Electronics: 15.68
Furniture: 15.50
Clothing: 15.29

This allows potential delays and category-level supply chain differences to be identified.

6. Backorder Analysis

Backorders are classified according to order status:

Fulfilled: 838
Pending: 248
Cancelled: 114

This provides visibility into the current status of delayed orders.

7. Inventory Level Analysis

Inventory levels are compared across four regions and four product categories to identify differences in inventory distribution and potential overstock/understock situations.
